FAQ

Everything you need to know about our pharmacy WHMIS certification. Find answers regarding course duration, compliance standards, re-takes, and how to get your certificate instantly.

How is this course different from others?

Our WHMIS course for pharmacy professionals is designed to address the unique hazards and materials commonly found in community and hospital pharmacies. While standard WHMIS training covers general workplace hazardous materials, our pharmacy-focused course emphasizes:

  • Common Pharmacy Hazards: Understanding exposure to disinfectants, alcohol-based sanitizers, compounding agents, and hazardous drugs such as cytotoxics.
  • Drug Handling & Compounding Safety: Safe preparation, labeling, and storage of pharmaceuticals and chemical reagents used in dispensing and compounding.
  • Biohazard Awareness: Procedures for managing contamination risks, handling sharps, and disposing of biohazardous waste safely.
  • Personal Protective Equipment (PPE): Proper selection and use of gloves, gowns, eye protection, and respirators specific to pharmacy environments.
  • Emergency Procedures: Steps for spill management, exposure response, and first aid in cases of chemical or drug-related incidents.

This course ensures that pharmacy professionals receive WHMIS training directly relevant to the materials, procedures, and safety challenges of their workplace.

How long does it take?

The course typically takes around 60 minutes to complete. Because it's 100% online, you can progress at your own pace and take breaks whenever you need.

Can I do a re-take exam?

Absolutely. We offer unlimited attempts to pass the final exam at no extra cost. We want you to feel confident in your knowledge of dental workplace safety.

When do I receive the certificate?

Certificates are issued instantly upon completion. As soon as you pass the exam, a high-resolution PDF certificate is generated and sent directly to your email.

How do we pay for the course?

Payment is handled through our secure online portal. You can purchase a single license for yourself or bulk packages for your entire dental clinic staff.

Does this course expire after purchase?

You have one full year from the date of purchase to complete the course. Once certified, the training is valid for one year, as per standard dental industry safety protocols.
